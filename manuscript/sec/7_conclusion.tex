\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \ours for dynamic mapping within and across visits. Scene-state intervals connect changes
in map geometry to changes in observation contributions: outdated geometry leaves the current map,
new observations reconstruct its replacement, and compatible earlier information continues to complete
unchanged regions. The same mechanism applies to TSDF, Gaussian, and occupancy reconstruction and retains
earlier states for historical queries. Experiments demonstrate more accurate dynamic reconstruction
across the three representations, greater completeness on partial revisits, and improved Gaussian rendering.
