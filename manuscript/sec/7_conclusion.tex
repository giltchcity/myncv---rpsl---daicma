\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \ours for dynamic mapping within and across visits. Scene-state intervals coordinate geometry
and observation updates: old geometry retires, new observations reconstruct changed states, and compatible
measurements complete unchanged regions. The same mechanism updates
TSDF, Gaussian, and occupancy maps while retaining past states. Experiments show gains in geometry,
completeness, and~\mbox{Gaussian rendering}.
