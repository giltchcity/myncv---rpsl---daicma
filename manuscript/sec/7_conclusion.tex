\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \ours, a unified method for dynamic mapping within and across visits.
Depth evidence identifies changes, and the inferred state intervals control both the geometry retained in the map
and the observations used to update it. TSDF, Gaussian, and occupancy maps apply this shared mechanism through
their native reconstruction procedures. They update changed objects, retain unobserved regions, and preserve earlier
states for historical queries. Experiments show improved reconstruction and change detection across representations,
with greater completeness on partial revisits.
