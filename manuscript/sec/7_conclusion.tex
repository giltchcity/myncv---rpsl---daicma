\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a method for continuing dense dynamic maps across separate robot
deployments. The system carries object states, background geometry, and scene
history into the next run, then uses new depth observations to refine, replace,
or remove imported states. On repeated real-room and synthetic experiments, the
method preserves more object coverage while removing substantially more obsolete
surface than the multi-session baseline. Public sequences show the same basic
behavior and expose the remaining difficulty of limited visibility and imperfect
background alignment. These results support persistent scene-state updates as a
practical extension of dense dynamic mapping to repeated deployments.
