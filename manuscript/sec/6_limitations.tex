\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The update depends on inferred object-state changes. A missed transition leaves
obsolete measurements eligible; a false transition can discard useful evidence.
Partial revisits can delay an absence decision, as for the removed 3RScan chair.

Temporal selection also does not correct pose misalignment. The real-room runs
use a rigid alignment, and residual local errors can place repeated wall
observations at different locations. The reported geometry results show the
resulting precision loss. Small visible motions remain subject to the backbone's
motion-extraction threshold, as in the Bonn box sequence.
