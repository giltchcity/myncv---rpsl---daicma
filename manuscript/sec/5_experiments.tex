\subsection{Experimental Setup}

\paragraph{Datasets and baselines.}
We use a real room (three visits), a simulated apartment and Flat~\cite{schmid2022panoptic} (two visits each),
and eight rescanned 3RScan rooms~\cite{wald2019rio}. Baselines are Khronos~\cite{schmid2024khronos} and
Panoptic Multi-TSDFs~\cite{schmid2022panoptic} for TSDF, GaME~\cite{yugay2026game} for Gaussians, and
wavemap~\cite{reijgwart2023wavemap} for occupancy. Methods process the same posed sequences and carry maps
across visits. TESSE additionally compares our TSDF with Khronos in its single-run 4D setting.


\paragraph{Metrics.}
Geometry uses surface F1 at 5\,cm before Gaussian refinement. Object F1 uses 0.5\,m matching and
duration-weighted snapshots; change F1 pools appearance/disappearance events across the chain.
3RScan averages geometry and object scores within rooms, then across rooms; events are pooled over all rooms.
Following GaME, every tenth frame of the final visit, except its first, is held out. After global refinement,
we report PSNR, MS-SSIM, and LPIPS; depth L1 uses pixels with measured depth. TESSE uses official aggregation
over map and query times.

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The shared tests use a 5\,cm surface tolerance and threshold $\ln 99$ across datasets.
Our TSDF and occupancy maps use 5\,cm cells. Controlled comparisons use annotated segmentation;
we also evaluate predicted masks.

\paragraph{Partial revisits.}
Continued maps preserve geometry left unobserved by a revisit (\cref{tab:restart}). On synthetic visit B,
recall improves over native restart by 21.1, 17.3, and 17.1 points for TSDF, Gaussians, and occupancy.

\paragraph{Rendering the updated scene.}
Our Gaussian maps improve all four rendering metrics on every dataset (\cref{tab:rendering}), gaining
0.96--3.43\,dB over GaME. On Flat, depth L1 decreases from 1.07 to 0.47\,cm. Observation reuse respects
changes while retaining compatible supervision.

\paragraph{Single-run 4D mapping.}
All four official F1 categories improve on both TESSE scenes (\cref{tab:tesse}). Change F1 increases from
43.6 to 79.0 in the office and from 36.9 to 92.7 in the apartment. This evaluation queries both current
and earlier states.

\subsection{Ablation Studies}

We ablate the shared mechanism using the Gaussian representation over Flat A$\rightarrow$B
(\cref{tab:ablation_variants}).

\paragraph{Change decisions.}
Removing change decisions reduces geometry F1 by 5.58 points, change F1 by 14.58 points, and PSNR by
3.84\,dB. Retiring outdated geometry benefits both detection and reconstruction.

\paragraph{Observation reuse.}
Unrestricted old frames reduce PSNR from 27.77 to 22.69\,dB and increase depth L1 from 0.45 to 3.66\,cm.
Discarding whole conflicting frames reaches similar PSNR on the extensively revisited Flat scene; selective
reuse achieves lower depth error and retains compatible observations.

\paragraph{Parameter sensitivity.}
Decision odds of $9$, $99$, and $999$ yield 27.67--27.81\,dB PSNR, with at most one event difference.
The fixed-prior sweep gives similar reconstruction scores.

\paragraph{Predicted segmentation.}
OneFormer~\cite{jain2023oneformer} predictions retain 89.89 geometry F1 and 27.56\,dB PSNR,
while object and change F1 reflect segmentation errors (\cref{tab:ablation_variants}).
