\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Implementation details.}
All datasets and maps share the constants of the tests: a 5\,cm support tolerance, a $60^\circ$ incidence
limit~\cite{nguyen2012kinect}, the threshold $\ln 99$ on $L_t$, at least $\min(30, \nu_{\mathrm{rel}})$ judged samples per
depth image, and a 10\,cm displacement distance for successors. The TSDF uses 5\,cm voxels and a truncation band of
$T = 10$\,cm, and the Gaussian map tests earlier Gaussians with $\tau_{\min} = 5$\,cm.
