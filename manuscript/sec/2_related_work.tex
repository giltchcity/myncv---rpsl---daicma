\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Dynamic reconstruction and object states.}
Co-Fusion, MID-Fusion, and EM-Fusion track and reconstruct objects with separate
surface or volume models~\cite{runz2017cofusion,xu2019midfusion,strecke2019emfusion}.
DynaSLAM~II and DynoSAM jointly estimate camera and object motion
~\cite{bescos2021dynaslam2,morris2025dynosam}. DYNEMO-SLAM incorporates moving
and relocated entities into a scene graph~\cite{giberna2025dynemo}, while
Lost~\&~Found reconstructs object trajectories during human--object interactions
~\cite{behrens2025lostfound}. Non-rigid and time-varying reconstruction extends
these representations through dynamic surfaces in 4DTAM
~\cite{matsuki2025fourdtam}, Gaussian primitives in 4D Gaussian Splatting SLAM
and DynaGSLAM~\cite{li2025fourdgsslam,li2026dynagslam}, and persistent rigid
primitives in 4D Primitive-Mache~\cite{mazur2026primitive}.
These methods provide geometry and motion estimates on which temporal map
maintenance can operate.

\paragraph{Changes revealed by later observations.}
Bore \etal model local object motion and larger state jumps
~\cite{bore2019movable}. Changing-SLAM maintains a Bayesian object-persistence
map~\cite{soares2023changing}. Khronos combines short-window motion tracking
with fragment association and ray-based verification of appearance and
disappearance~\cite{schmid2024khronos}. LTC-Mapping accumulates visibility and
non-detection evidence~\cite{matezbandera2022ltc}; SuperMap maintains object
occupancy, semantic confidence, and temporal relations~\cite{zhao2026supermap}.
GaME adds supported geometry and removes contradicted Gaussian primitives in
an evolving scene~\cite{yugay2026game}. Our formulation connects such temporal
state decisions to reconstruction: a change determines which earlier
observations remain eligible to define the current geometry.

\paragraph{Updating maps across visits.}
Long-term map maintenance revises earlier geometry as an environment changes
~\cite{pomerleau2014longterm,lazaro2018efficient}. Dynamic Pose Graph SLAM
maintains scan geometry and a history of added and removed points
~\cite{walcott2012dynamic}. LT-Mapper separates alignment, filtering, and
map-change management~\cite{kim2022ltmapper}; RBIF uses ray-bundle evidence
for occupancy updates~\cite{breitfuss2024rbif}. ELite updates point-wise
ephemerality~\cite{gil2025elite}, ProbPer-LiLo refines maps after filtering
non-static content~\cite{ali2026probperlilo}, and LT-Gaussian removes
disappeared Gaussian centers and adds emerging geometry
~\cite{cheng2025ltgaussian}.

Object-centric approaches preserve additional structure. Panoptic Multi-TSDFs
maintains object and background volumes with persistent, absent, and unobserved
states~\cite{schmid2022panoptic}. POCD models object change and stationarity,
and POV-SLAM couples object consistency with pose estimation
~\cite{qian2022pocd,qian2023povslam}. ObVi-SLAM transfers object landmarks
~\cite{adkins2024obvislam}; OASIS-Map associates objects across sessions
~\cite{oh2026oasismap}; Living Scenes matches and reconstructs objects from
scans acquired at different times~\cite{zhu2024livingscenes}. These works
establish the importance of prior maps, association, and observation coverage.
We organize the update around two explicit selections: the temporal validity of
measurements and the spatial eligibility of memory.

\paragraph{Temporal persistence and scene memory.}
Occupancy-grid HMMs learn state-transition probabilities
~\cite{meyerdelius2012changinggrid}, FreMEn represents periodic environmental
changes~\cite{krajnik2017fremen}, and Spatio-Temporal Hilbert Maps model
continuous time-dependent occupancy~\cite{senanayake2016sthm}. Perpetua
maintains multiple hypotheses for feature appearance and disappearance
~\cite{saavedraruiz2025perpetua}. DAAAM adds detailed semantic descriptions to
a hierarchical 4D scene graph~\cite{gorlo2025daaam}, and LT-Mem stores current
object states, session-level events, and long-term statistics
~\cite{lee2026ltmem}. Our use of temporal state is directed at map construction:
the history determines which observations may contribute to current geometry,
while ended states remain available as history.
